The booking process

From search to confirmed
in four steps.

No agency fees. No phone tag. No uncertainty. Search, choose, request — and receive a personalised quote from a verified professional within 24 hours.

1
Search with your details

Enter your occasion, talent type, date, location, language and budget. Results show only verified professionals available on your specific date.

2
Browse profiles & rates

View full profiles — experience, photos, reviews, rate ranges, languages and availability calendar. Compare as many as you like at no cost.

3
Send a tentative booking request

Click "Book Tentatively" on any profile. Your request goes directly to the professional via a verified secure link. No payment, no commitment — just a reservation of interest.

4
Receive your personalised quote

Within 24 hours, the professional contacts you with a personalised quote based on your event details. You then confirm, negotiate or decline — entirely your choice.

Typical rate ranges across Sri Lanka. Actual quotes depend on experience level, event duration, location and specific requirements. All quotes are personalised and provided within 24 hours of a tentative booking request.

These are market ranges — not fixed prices. Entry-level professionals start at the low end; award-winning or internationally experienced professionals command the high end. Overseas events (Australia, UK, UAE) will attract travel and accommodation costs in addition. Final pricing is always confirmed directly with the professional.

Common questions

Everything you need to know

A tentative booking is a reservation of interest — not a contract. When you click "Book Tentatively" and fill in the form, your details go directly to the professional via a verified secure link. The professional responds within 24 hours with a personalised quote and confirms their availability. You then decide whether to proceed. No money changes hands until you actively confirm the booking directly with the professional.
Your booking request is sent to the professional via a verified platform link. They will contact you directly on the email address and WhatsApp number you provided in the form — typically within a few hours, always within 24 hours. The conversation then continues directly between you and the professional — we facilitate the introduction, not the negotiation.
The rates shown on each profile (From / Up to) are indicative ranges based on that professional's typical pricing. The final quote is personalised to your specific event — date, location, duration, guest count and any special requirements all affect the final price. This is why the tentative booking request asks for event details: so the professional can give you an accurate, customised quote rather than a generic number.
Absolutely. Many events need multiple professionals — a photographer and a videographer, an MC and a band, a planner and a florist. You can send tentative booking requests to as many professionals as you need, all at no cost. Each professional responds independently with their quote, and you choose who to confirm with.
The platform is specifically designed for diaspora event organisers. Many professionals on the platform are based overseas themselves (Australia, UK, UAE) and are familiar with planning cross-border events. For Sri Lanka-based events being organised from abroad, professionals are experienced with video call consultations, digital contracts and remote coordination. Simply include your country in the location field and note that you are organising remotely in your booking request.
Every professional listed on Sri Lankan Event Portal has been reviewed by our team before their profile goes live. We check their professional experience, request references from past events, confirm their identity, and review portfolio samples. Professionals with a "Verified" badge have passed this process. We also monitor reviews from event organisers who have used them through the platform — consistent negative feedback results in review and potential removal.
No. Searching, browsing profiles, comparing professionals and sending tentative booking requests are all completely free for event organisers. The platform is funded by the professionals who list their services — not by the people searching for them. You pay only what you agree directly with the professional after receiving their personalised quote.
If you have not heard from a professional within 24 hours of your tentative booking request, contact us at info@srilankaneventportal.com and we will follow up on your behalf. All professionals on the platform commit to 24-hour response times as a condition of their listing. Persistent non-responsiveness results in their profile being suspended.
